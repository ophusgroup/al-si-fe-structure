# Relation to the Literature

The Al-Fe-Si system contains roughly twelve stable ternary phases, labeled $\tau_1$ through $\tau_{12}$, plus several metastable ones. The table below lists the phases nearest our measured composition, after Marker et al. [](#marker2011) with homogeneity ranges from Krendelsberger et al. [](#krendelsberger2007).

| Phase | Formula | Composition range (at.%) | Structure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\tau_2$ | Al$_3$FeSi | Fe 19.5–20.5, Si 15.2–25.6 | monoclinic |
| $\tau_3$ | Al$_2$FeSi | Fe 23.5–24.5, Si 20.5–22.5 | $Cmma$, oC128 |
| $\tau_4$ | Al$_3$FeSi$_2$ | Fe 15.5–16.5, Si 30.5–38 | $I4/mcm$, tI24 |
| $\tau_6$ ($\beta$) | Al$_{4.5}$FeSi | Fe 15.5–16.5, Si 17–19 | $A2/a$, mC52 |
| $\tau_7$ | Al$_3$Fe$_2$Si$_3$ | Fe 23.5–24.5, Si 27.8–36.3 | $P2_1/n$, mP64 |

Our measured composition (Al 52.6, Si 29.4, Fe 18.3) sits in the $\tau_4$ field, about 2 atomic percent rich in Fe relative to the reported range. The measured lattice, the projected cells along eight zone axes, and the tilt geometry all confirm the $\tau_4$ assignment; no other phase in the system indexes even a majority of the observed orientations.

## Naming

The same phase appears in the literature under several names. $\tau_4$, $\delta$-Al$_4$FeSi$_2$, and Al$_3$FeSi$_2$ all refer to the tetragonal $I4/mcm$ structure; Fang et al. write it (Al$_{1-x}$Si$_x$)$_5$Fe. The $\beta$ phase of casting metallurgy is $\tau_6$, written variously Al$_{4.5}$FeSi, Al$_5$FeSi, or Al$_9$Fe$_2$Si$_2$.

## Prior structural work on $\tau_4$

Guéneau et al. [](#gueneau1995) solved the average structure by single-crystal X-ray diffraction and gave both the $I4/mcm$ description with a mixed Al/Si site and an ordered $Pbcn$ description. Fang et al. [](#fang2025) computed the phase from first principles: the $Pbcn$ ordering is the ground state, the order-disorder temperature is near 660 K, and the Al/Si antisite cost is 0.07 to 0.10 eV, which explains both the wide composition range and the weak, short-range ordering we observe.

Becker et al. [](#becker2019) showed that $\beta$ and $\delta$ ($\tau_4$) are built from closely related structural blocks, intergrow on shared planes, and form polytypes. Their work is the closest precedent for the defect structure here: our particle shows the same tendency toward planar disorder, expressed as (001) antiphase boundaries within domains and near-twin orientation domains between them. Hansen et al. [](#hansen1998) performed the corresponding faulted-structure analysis for $\beta$ by diffraction and high-resolution imaging, and their methodology is the model for the analysis on this site.

## What is new here

Two results on this site are not, to our knowledge, in the published record. First, the direct multi-zone-axis confirmation of the $\tau_4$ structure in a single particle by atomic resolution imaging, including quantitative agreement of eight projected cells. Second, the identification of two τ₄ orientation domains within one particle, related by an approximately 90 degree switch of the tetragonal axis on the shared pseudo-cubic Fe subcell. The domain B lattice was reconstructed from the imaging data alone and verified against the goniometer geometry to a fraction of a degree. The pseudo-cubic subcell makes such ferroelastic domains natural, and we expect them to be a general feature of τ₄ particles in castings.
