# Evidence

The assignment rests on four independent measurements: the lattice geometry of the mean unit cells, the goniometer tilt angles, the reconstruction of the second domain's lattice with no structure assumed, and the pixel-level agreement between simulated and experimental projections. This page collects the quantitative comparisons and the alternatives that were tested and rejected.

## Pixel calibration

Indexing the domain A zone axes against the published τ₄ cell gives six independent lattice spacings across three orientations. All six require the same correction: the recorded pixel sizes are 4.6 percent too large. The ratio of model to measured spacing is $0.9563 \pm 0.0014$ across all six, a 0.15 percent internal consistency, so we treat this as a magnification calibration error and apply the factor 0.9563 throughout. The alternative reading, a real lattice 4.6 percent larger than the published cell, is not plausible for an intermetallic.

## Indexing domain A

Five mean unit cells index on one τ₄ orientation. Measured cells are drift corrected and calibrated; model cells are the translation nets of the projected atom columns, which for some zones are finer than the projected Bravais lattice.

| Dataset | Zone | Measured cell (Å, deg) | Model cell (Å, deg) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ZA1_3 | $[110]$ | 4.281 × 4.767, 89.9 | 4.286 × 4.763, 90.0 |
| ZA2_1 | $[130]$ | 1.911 × 4.780, 90.1 | 1.917 × 4.763, 90.0 |
| ZA3_1 | $[010]$ | 3.031 × 4.767, 90.2 | 3.031 × 4.763, 90.0 |
| ZA4_1 | $[210]$ | 2.705 × 9.540, 89.7 | 2.711 × 9.525, 89.9 |
| ZA5_2 | $[010]$ | 3.028 × 4.769, 89.9 | 3.031 × 4.763, 90.0 |

All errors are below 0.4 percent in length and 0.3 degrees in angle. The goniometer provides an independent check: the exact stage tilts recorded in the microscope metadata place all five beams within 0.25 degrees of one plane, the zone band perpendicular to $c$, and the fitted crystal orientation predicts each recorded tilt to better than one degree. The raw scans in the ZA3 folder, recorded at $\alpha = 10.9°$, sit within half a degree of where this fit predicts the $[120]$ zone, a confirmed prediction on data that was never used in the fit.

## Reconstructing domain B

The three remaining beams lie on a second band, coplanar to 0.04 degrees, and do not correspond to any zone of domain A. We reconstructed the lattice of domain B directly from the data: the ZA7 and ZA9 mean cells define two reciprocal lattice planes with known absolute orientations, and requiring both to belong to one three dimensional lattice determines that lattice with no candidate structure assumed. The result:

| | Reconstructed | τ₄ primitive cell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reduced cell (Å) | 6.107, 6.107, 6.369 | 6.061, 6.061, 6.405 |
| Angles (deg) | 61.8, 61.7, 90.3 | 61.7, 61.7, 90.0 |
| Volume (Å³) | 176.1 | 174.9 |

Domain B is τ₄ to better than one percent. With its orientation fixed by the reconstruction, the three beams fall on low index zones:

| Dataset | Zone (domain B) | Beam error | Measured cell (Å, deg) | Model cell (Å, deg) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ZA7_1 | $[33\bar{1}]$ | 0.38° | 2.949 × 8.671, 89.9 | 2.976 × 8.574, 89.9 |
| ZA8_1 | $[221]$ | 0.12° | 2.077 × 4.333, 92.6 | 2.080 × 4.289, 89.9 |
| ZA9_1 | $[331]$ | 0.12° | 8.649 × 2.963, 90.0 | 8.574 × 2.976, 89.9 |

The ZA7 and ZA9 cells are centred and agree with each other to 0.3 percent, as required for the mirror pair $[33\bar{1}]$ and $[331]$. The domain B cell dimensions run 0.6 to 1.0 percent above ideal, which we attribute to strain. The ZA8 angle anomaly of 2.6 degrees, together with the fact that ZA8 matches simulation only after mirroring, points to residual scan distortion in that dataset (see the [ZA8 page](../meanuc/za8.md)).

## Simulated projections

The final check is the projected intensity itself. For each mean unit cell we simulate the projection of the τ₄ model along the assigned zone, with Fe columns weighted 3.0 times Al/Si following the $Z^{1.7}$ scaling of HAADF contrast, and fit only a Gaussian blur width. Correlation coefficients:

| ZA1 | ZA2 | ZA3 | ZA4 | ZA5 | ZA7 | ZA8 | ZA9 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.95 | 0.99 | 0.98 | 0.97 | 0.98 | 1.00 | 0.97 | 0.99 |

The per-zone comparisons are in the [mean unit cell module](../meanuc/index.md), and every panel is generated by the analysis script in the repository.

## Could one crystal explain all nine beams?

We checked whether the ZA7, ZA8, and ZA9 beams could be zone axes of the domain A lattice, so that a single crystal would explain the whole dataset. They cannot, at three levels. The nearest low index zones (all indices up to 6) miss the measured beams by 5.3 to 7.2 degrees, far outside the degree-level alignment that lattice imaging requires. Reaching the beams within tolerance requires zones like $[23\,12\,\bar{2}]$ or $[30\,22\,\bar{3}]$, whose lattice repeat along the beam is 160 to 230 Å; projected columns along such directions contain almost no repeating atoms and cannot produce the strong, clean lattice images observed, let alone match low index simulations at $r$ near 0.99. And a supercell does not help: a supercell is a coarser translation lattice, so its rational directions are a subset of those already tested. The domain B assignment, by contrast, places all three beams on zones with 13 to 27 Å repeats, at 0.12 to 0.38 degrees.

## Alternatives tested

The following competing hypotheses were tested quantitatively and rejected, or found indistinguishable:

| Hypothesis | Test | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Any other Al-Fe-Si phase (β, λ, τ₁, τ₇, τ₈, α, Fe₈Al₁₇Si₈, Al, Si) | index all eight cells + tilt geometry | no phase indexes more than a minority of zones |
| Domain B is a different phase | structure-free lattice reconstruction | reconstructed cell is τ₄ to 1 percent |
| High index zones of a single crystal | rational-zone search along measured beams | no zone of domain A within tolerance |
| Sample remount between sessions | acquisition timestamps | one continuous session, ZA1 through ZA9 |
| Fe on the $4c$ site instead of $4a$ | image simulation, all zones | correlation collapses (ZA4 falls to 0.63) |
| Ordered $Pbcn$ vs disordered $I4/mcm$ | image simulation | indistinguishable ($\Delta r < 0.01$); ordering visible only as diffuse scattering |
| Refined $16l$ position | free $(x, z)$ fit against the sensitive zones | optimum within 0.1 Å of published values |
| Fe/(Al,Si) contrast ratio | global weight scan | best at 3.0, matching $Z^{1.7}$ |

The one measurement that does not close cleanly is the domain boundary crystallography, discussed on the [model page](model.md): the misorientation is near, but not exactly, a ferroelastic twin operation.
