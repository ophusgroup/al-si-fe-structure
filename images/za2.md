# ZA2

| | |
| --- | --- |

The widest field of view in the series at atomic resolution. The narrow 1.9 Å transverse repeat makes this orientation the most sensitive to residual drift, and the strong diffuse streaking along the vertical fringes reflects the (001) fault population.
