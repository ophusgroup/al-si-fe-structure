The registered image in this folder duplicates the ZA5_1 field of view; the tilt values above are therefore those of the ZA5 acquisition. The raw scans actually recorded in the ZA3 folder were taken at alpha 10.94, beta -2.20 degrees, which the orientation fit identifies as the parent [120] zone. Those scans await drift correction.
