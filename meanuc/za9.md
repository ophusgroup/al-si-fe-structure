# ZA9: [331] domain B

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Goniometer | $\alpha = -15.33°$, $\beta = -11.16°$ |
| Domain, zone axis | domain B, $[331]$ |
| Measured cell | 8.649 × 2.963 Å, 90.0° |
| Model cell | 8.574 × 2.976 Å, 89.9° |
| Fitted blur, correlation | $\sigma = 0.75$ Å, $r = 0.99$ |

The $[331]$ zone of domain B, the mirror image of [ZA7](za7.md) through the domain B band axis. The projected cell is centred; the parity analysis of the mean cell gives the centring translation to four decimal places, the cleanest centring in the series. The characteristic wavy rows of bright columns in this projection are reproduced well by the simulation.

The beam sits 8.8 degrees from the ZA8 orientation and 40.2 degrees from ZA7, matching the ideal domain B zone geometry to 0.1 degrees in both cases.
