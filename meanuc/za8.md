| Fitted blur, correlation | $\sigma = 0.80$ Å, $r = 0.97$ (mirrored) |

The $[221]$ zone of domain B. The 4.33 Å repeat is half the twin $[1\bar{1}0]$ period, and the measured lengths match the Fe column net of the model to 0.2 and 1.0 percent.

Two anomalies are specific to this dataset. First, the experimental cell only matches the simulation after mirroring. Several registered images in the series carry arbitrary frame handedness, so a mirror by itself is not alarming, but it should be kept in mind when comparing this page against the others. Second, the measured cell angle of 92.6 degrees deviates from the model value of 89.9 degrees by 2.6 degrees, by far the largest angle error in the series, and the mirror does not remove it. The likely cause is residual drift distortion that the 0/90 degree registration did not fully cancel. The tiled comparison below shows the resulting shear: the experimental bright features are elongated diagonally while the simulated ones are symmetric.
